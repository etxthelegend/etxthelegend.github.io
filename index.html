Mechanical Engineering (Aerospace) · UBC

Soot, flames, and the flows that carry them.

I'm a fourth-year Mechanical Engineering student at the University of British Columbia, specializing in aerospace. I work in the UBC Energy & Aerosol Laboratory with Prof. Steven Rogak, where I build deep-learning tools that measure soot particles in electron-microscope images. I'm heading toward graduate research in gas-turbine combustion, sustainable aviation fuels, and the emissions they produce.

Research interests

Research →
  1. 01

    Gas-turbine combustion & turbomachinery

    How combustor design, operating conditions, and fuel properties shape efficiency, operability, flame stability, heat transfer, pressure loss, and overall propulsion performance.

  2. 02

    Sustainable aviation fuels, soot & emissions

    How conventional and alternative jet fuels change soot/nvPM formation, gaseous emissions, particle morphology, and fuel-specific combustion behaviour in propulsion-relevant systems.

  3. 03

    Turbulent reacting flows, CFD & aerosol diagnostics

    Computational and experimental study of turbulent mixing, particle-laden flows, particle sampling, and deep-learning image processing for particle measurement.

Research2026

CarboSAMY

A deep-learning workflow that finds, segments, and measures soot and other carbonaceous particles in electron-microscope images, reducing analysis time by 99.6%.

Design & build2025–26

Smart Brake Chamber

A motorized locking device that keeps thieves from releasing a trailer's parking brakes through the caging port, while staying quick to remove for authorized maintenance.

Analysis2025

Particle Image Velocimetry over an Airfoil

PIV in a recirculating water channel to map the flow around a NACA 0012 from ~1° to ~38° angle of attack, and estimate lift from circulation.
