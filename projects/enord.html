ENORD

Fully 3D-printed RC cave crawler

Year
2024–
Context
Personal project
Tools
CAD · 3D printing · Suspension design

An RC crawler designed from scratch to handle real cave terrain, with a 4-link suspension and an almost entirely 3D-printed chassis.

Targets

  • Traverse uneven cave terrain with high-articulation suspension
  • About 3 hours of run time on battery
  • At least 10 m/s on flat ground
  • Fully 3D-printed and easy to repair
  • Room for LEDs and a small camera

Design

A 4-link suspension with generous wheel travel, geometry tuned to keep wheels planted on sharp terrain, and a modular chassis. Parts are PLA+ and PETG with tuned orientation, infill, and wall thickness, plus metal inserts at high-load points.

Status

First prototype built and tested; suspension meets expectations. Next: camera and lighting for cave trials, dust and moisture protection, and TPU bumpers and tires.
