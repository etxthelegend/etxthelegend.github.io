LES Subgrid-Scale Model Review

Eddy-viscosity closures for wall-bounded turbulence

Year
2026
Context
MECH 586 · Turbulent Shear Flow
Tools
Turbulence · LES · CFD
Read the review (PDF)

A technical review comparing Smagorinsky, dynamic Smagorinsky, WALE, Vreman, and Sigma models, with a focus on WALE for wall-bounded flows.

Scope

Compared the assumptions behind five eddy-viscosity closures for large-eddy simulation: Smagorinsky, dynamic Smagorinsky, WALE, Vreman, and Sigma.

Focus

Evaluated near-wall scaling, sensitivity to strain and rotation rates, and suitability for wall-bounded turbulent shear flows, with an emphasis on the WALE model.

Findings

WALE uses the square of the resolved velocity-gradient tensor, so it responds to both strain and rotation and recovers the correct cubic near-wall scaling of eddy viscosity without a damping function. Its weaknesses are a fixed model constant, a non-zero response in solid-body rotation, and less consistent behaviour in some under-resolved simulations. Those weaknesses motivated dynamic WALE formulations and the σ-model. WALE is not universally optimal, but it remains a useful model and the basis for later wall-adapting closures.
