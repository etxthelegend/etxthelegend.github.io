STOODIS

High-power solid-motor rocket · Thunderbird Aerospace

Year
2023–25
Context
Thunderbird Aerospace · First Nations Launch 2024
Tools
SolidWorks · Lathe · FEA · Composites

A 180 cm fibreglass-and-aluminium rocket for the 2024 First Nations Launch. I designed the fin can and built the von Kármán nosecone that housed the payload.

Mission

Stable ascent on an HP-K535 motor, dual-deployment recovery (drogue at apogee, main at low altitude), a secure sensor payload, and post-flight comparison against predictions.

My work

  • Led the fin-can design, including fin geometry, retention, and interfaces to the motor mount, bulkheads, and airframe
  • Computed the von Kármán profile, modelled and machined the nosecone, and designed the internal payload, sensor, and battery mounts
  • Machined aluminium fuel-tank features on the lathe to tight tolerances
  • Ran heat-transfer analysis near the nozzle of a liquid-propellant rocket to size the ablative liner
  • Repaired transport damage to the nosecone before launch

Flight

Stoodis flew stable with correct drogue and main deployment, reaching about 2,200 ft by GPS against 3,534 ft predicted. An eyebolt failure caused partial separation on recovery, but every structure I built came back intact.
