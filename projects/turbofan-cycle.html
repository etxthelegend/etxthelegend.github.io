Mixed Turbofan Cycle Analysis

Zero-dimensional MATLAB engine model

Year
2025
Context
MECH 477 · Aerospace Propulsion
Tools
MATLAB · Propulsion · Thermodynamics

A station-by-station cycle model of a mixed-flow turbofan, used to study how bypass ratio, pressure ratio, and turbine inlet temperature trade thrust against fuel burn.

Model

A zero-dimensional MATLAB model applying mass, energy, and momentum balances at each engine station, from inlet through fan, compressor, combustor, turbines, mixer, and nozzle.

Parametric studies

Swept bypass ratio, compressor pressure ratio, turbine inlet temperature, and component efficiencies to see their effect on thrust, specific fuel consumption, and overall propulsion performance.
