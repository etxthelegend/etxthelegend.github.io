Research

Measuring the particles that combustion leaves behind.

UBC Energy & Aerosol Laboratory · Supervisor: Prof. Steven Rogak · May 2026 – Aug 2026

Deep-learning segmentation of soot in TEM images

Built CarboSAMY, an automated workflow that detects, segments, and measures the morphology of soot and other carbonaceous particles in transmission electron microscopy images, cutting analysis time by 99.6% compared with manual analysis. Manuscript submitted.

Project page →

Thermophoretic TEM sampler redesign

Led the redesign of a thermophoretic sampler for field aerosol collection, using Ansys Fluent to evaluate jet temperature gradients, nozzle velocity, and downstream thermal behaviour for better particle deposition on TEM grids.

Mobile jet-engine exhaust duct

Designed a gusseted support frame for a mobile jet-engine exhaust duct with a ~12 m vertical riser, using CAD and FEA for the structure and MATLAB air-entrainment and heat-transfer models to size the duct and estimate mixed-gas temperatures.

Mentoring the TEM annotation team

Supervise two students doing manual segmentation of soot TEM images; coordinate annotation, review segmentation consistency, and guide mask preparation for model-validation data.
